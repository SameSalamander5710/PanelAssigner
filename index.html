PanelAssigner

Simple optimizer for flow cytometry panel design that assigns fluorophores to antibodies
and ranks panel configurations by spectral complexity.

Based on configuration: Cytek Aurora 5L 16UV-16V-14B-10YG-8R For more details, see: https://github.com/SameSalamander5710/PanelAssigner.

Settings

iScore used to rank panels. All are computed from the similarity matrix of the panel; lower is better and 1 means perfectly distinct spectra. Condition index: square root of the largest over the smallest eigenvalue. A-optimality: average variance inflation (mean of 1/eigenvalue). Every overlapping fluorophore counts. D-optimality: total information (determinant based, geometric mean of 1/eigenvalue). Rewards the overall spread of the spectra. E-optimality: worst-case inflation (1/smallest eigenvalue). Only the most overlapping combination counts. After Compute, the same metric is used to rank suggested additions.
